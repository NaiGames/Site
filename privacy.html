Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 4, 2024

Last Updated: November 28, 2025

Scope of this Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to all games in the N3 Shooter Series, developed and published by a single independent developer. The series includes:

  • M1 – Mission 1 – First Contact (Power Shield)
  • M2 – Mission 2 – Squad Operation (Energy Bomb)
  • M3 – Mission 3 – In the Dark (Power Generator Parts)
  • M4 – Mission 4 – Code Red (Invisibility Power)
  • M5 – Mission 5 – Operation Recovery (Stolen Technology)
  • M6 – Mission 6 – Operation Destruction (The War Is Here)

All games in the series share the same backend services, login system (PlayFab), and advertising integrations. The data handling practices described below apply equally to all titles.

1. Information We Collect

Our games may automatically collect information through third-party services, depending on the mission and ad networks used:

  • M1 – Mission 1: Unity Ads / LevelPlay, Chartboost, ironSource (if used)
  • M2 and onwards: Appodeal (mediates multiple ad networks)
  • Device identifiers (e.g., advertising ID), cookies, or similar technologies.
  • Technical information: IP address, device type, language, and performance metrics (crash reports, diagnostics).
  • Usage data: Interactions, session duration, in-game events, game progress, login data, and leaderboard activity via PlayFab.

2. How We Use Information

  • Serve relevant and, where applicable, personalized ads.
  • Analyze game performance and improve user experience.
  • Prevent fraud and abuse and ensure secure operation of the game.

3. Legal Basis & Consent

Where required by law, we request your consent for personalized advertising. You can manage your preferences in-game (if available) or through your device settings.

4. Sharing with Third Parties

We share data with service providers necessary to operate the game and deliver ads. The providers vary by mission:

  • M1 – Mission 1: Unity Ads, Chartboost, ironSource
  • M2 and onwards: Appodeal (which may share data with its mediated networks)
  • PlayFab (Microsoft) for game backend and leaderboards

5. Data Retention

We retain your data only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described above and to comply with legal obligations.

6. User Rights & Contact

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data by contacting: 📩 soporte.n3shooterseries@gmail.com

We process all requests in accordance with applicable laws, including GDPR, CCPA, and LGPD.

7. Children’s Privacy

This game targets a general audience and is not intended for children under the digital age of consent. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children without parental consent where required by law.

8. Third-Party Privacy Policies

For advertising purposes, we use third-party networks. Their privacy policies govern their use of your information:

  • Unity Ads: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Chartboost: https://www.chartboost.com/privacy
  • ironSource: https://www.ironsrc.com/privacy-policy/
  • Appodeal: https://www.appodeal.com/privacy-policy/
  • PlayFab (Microsoft): https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/servicesagreement
